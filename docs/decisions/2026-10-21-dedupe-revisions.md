# Task 13 — dedupe/revision implementation and decisions awaiting approval

**Status: provisional implementation, not an approved matching or resend policy.** Task 13 remains subject to human verification. No production ingestion or delivery wiring was changed.

## Implemented, testable behavior

- Identity uses family, source, native id when present; markets instead use source, instrument, interval and **provider observation time**. Market fetch time and price do not enter the key. Missing market observation time/instrument/interval fails closed instead of fabricating a key.
- Without a native id, other families use source plus normalized URL and published time; if no URL, normalized title and published time. If publication time is absent, fetch time is used as a provisional fallback; repeat fetches at different times may become different identities. Identity calculation does not decide D3 eligibility. Missing/future provider times must be excluded from delivery by the separate D3 selector; a stable identity is not proof of eligibility.
- Revision snapshots include title, summary, severity, alert and market price/quote unit. Corrections to those fields produce another monotonic revision count; unchanged observations retain the count. SQLite serializes concurrent upserts. SQLite deliveries are indexed by identity, explicit subscriber id, channel id and revision count/fingerprint; acknowledgments require the exact current persisted revision, preventing arbitrary fingerprints or late older-send acknowledgments from suppressing a newer revision. Reopening the database suppresses an acknowledged repeat for the same subscriber/channel. Sending is **not** performed here.
- News clusters use complete-link matching (not transitive bridge union), with existing provisional rules: same normalized URL, or exact normalized distinctive title from different sources; both require publication timestamps no more than six hours apart. Similar titles remain separate. Existing alias keys are never rewritten when a lexically earlier source arrives. URLs are comparison keys only, not pages to fetch.

## Decisions for the parent and human, **not approved**

1. **News matching:** Approve/change/reject the inherited six-hour window, four-word / twenty-character exact-title minimum, URL normalization (lowercased paths and dropped query parameters), and whether equal URLs with missing publication times can be matched. No fuzzy similarity threshold was introduced. A reused URL within six hours may still falsely merge distinct stories. A reusable URL after six hours stays separate. Existing clusters from different polls are not automatically re-evaluated together; inputs must be supplied together. If a pre-existing alias points to a different canonical cluster, immutable aliases avoid rewrites but can leave split clusters; migration/merge policy needs direction.
2. **Revision resend after revert:** Current per-revision proposal treats A→B→A as revision 3 and pending again, even when fingerprint A was delivered earlier. This is characterized in tests, **not approval** to resend it. Choose resend per new revision vs suppress previously delivered material for each subscriber/channel; decide before production delivery integration.
3. **Task 19 boundary:** The delivery/queue owner must provide subscriber and channel IDs and an immutable revision token and must implement crash-safe send/ack coordination for D5. SQLite ack suppression alone cannot guarantee exactly-once sending across a crash between external send and acknowledgment. No host integration exists in this task.
4. **Source-store corrections:** `NewsStore`, `DisasterStore`, `MarketStore` and `AdvisoryStore` use `ON CONFLICT ... DO NOTHING` and can discard provider corrections before task 13 observes them. These stores were read only under this task's scope; update/wiring needs separate authorization.
5. **Missing ids/time:** Fetch-time fallback is not stable across polls with missing publication time, and title-only keys can split on headline correction or collide for unrelated stories. Decide whether a persistent first-seen association is required; do not consider fetch time provider observation time or use it to make a D3-ineligible item eligible.
