# Source integration research — adapter notes, not validated coverage

This is read-only planning evidence for Deliverable 3 tasks 05 (news), 07 (disasters) and 09 (markets). Three bounded DeepSeek investigations used the existing samples in `docs/requirement-coverage.md`; **none made a new provider request**. The observations below are not operational ingestion, worldwide validation or confirmation of 24-hour historical availability. Event-record design and matching decisions belong to tasks 01 and 04; integration belongs to later tasks 06, 08 and 10.

| Family | Evidence already sampled | Candidate adapter output for later review | Outstanding source validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World news and consequential topics | BBC, NYT, Al Jazeera and Guardian world RSS returned dated article links/titles; Ars Technica returned AI/security items, Nature returned science publications. No acquisition item was verified. | Source identity and native GUID/link, original link/title, publication time, feed and item categories when present, source attribution, first-seen time; do not manufacture an event-occurrence time or region from editorial feeds. Group repeat articles across outlets only after a reviewed deduplication rule. | RSS snapshots may omit earlier stories; 24-hour startup recall, topic/geographic completeness, language bias, event versus analysis, attribution and reuse terms unverified. A research paper is not automatically a multi-year breakthrough. |
| Major disasters and health | GDACS sampled earthquake, wildfire and flood with event ID/type, country, alert level/score, location and CAP link; EONET returned a cyclone with geometry/category/magnitude/source; WHO returned outbreak metadata, IFRC a country-tagged flood/severity. USGS remains a lead only. | Source/native ID, normalized event type, country and optional geometry, provider-specific severity with its scale, known event/publication/update times and original source/CAP links. Keep absent fields absent rather than guessing. Multiple providers may describe the same event. | Major types and regions not covered by samples, source-overlap rules, worldwide timeliness, occurrence timestamps and 24-hour history remain unverified. No live sample established all major hazards in all regions. |
| Market observations | Binance sampled a BTCUSDT 24-hour ticker; Frankfurter sampled a daily EUR/USD rate; an undocumented Yahoo chart returned one S&P 500 history sample. | Instrument identifier, venue/source, unit/base/quote, numeric value and *observation* timestamp distinct from fetch time, provenance and possible stale-data indicator. Distinguish rolling-window ticker aggregate, daily FX reference and index levels; movement comparisons need actual historical observations. | No documented dependable worldwide equity/index or broad multi-asset coverage, verified historical 24-hour replay, freshness, rights, cross-region instruments or scoring criteria. A 24-hour Binance ticker is not automatically a replayable series, and the Yahoo response is not a validated product API. |

**Shared integration boundary proposed for review:** one source-specific adapter per feed or observation family produces normalized records with original provenance; preserve provider-native metadata needed by deterministic importance decisions, then persist observations for deduplication and startup catch-up. These are suggestions, not approved field names, algorithms or thresholds. A late-published item is eligible for a normal notification under the agreed product direction, subject to the as-yet-undecided timestamp and pacing rules.

**Research discipline:** GDELT, ReliefWeb v1, the probed Meteoalarm endpoint and Stooq remain blacklisted; none was contacted. Any future provider refusing a probe must be blacklisted and recorded in `docs/requirement-coverage.md` without retry. No conclusion here changes the human-approved worldwide goal or the requirement that event-data sources need no account/API key.
