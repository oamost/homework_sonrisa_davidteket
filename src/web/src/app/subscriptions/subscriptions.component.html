Alert setup

All approved types are selected initially. Remove or re-add tags and save once; the saved choice applies to every configured channel. Only events assessed as important are eligible. New items published within the previous 24 hours may qualify even if the event happened earlier; items with no provider time or a future timestamp do not qualify.

Email and Slack are ready to deliver as soon as their private settings and destination are saved. Existing legacy subscriptions remain respected; disabled legacy rows stay paused. Startup catch-up is bounded to 50, then 10 sends per minute. Provider coverage varies; worldwide and continuous coverage is not proven.
